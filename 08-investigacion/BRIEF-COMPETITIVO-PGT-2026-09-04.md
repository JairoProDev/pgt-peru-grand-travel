## 2. Peru Hop — adjacent, not a substitute

Peru Hop is a hop-on hop-off **bus** (Lima–Paracas–Huacachina–Arequipa–Puno–Cusco), not a Machu Picchu operator.

- How it works: [https://bushop.com/peru/how-it-works/](https://bushop.com/peru/how-it-works/)
- Independent explainers: [Huacachina.com 2026 guide](https://www.huacachina.com/travel-tips/what-is-peru-hop-and-how-does-it-work-updated-guide/), [How to Peru checklist](https://howtoperu.com/answers/peru-hop-booking-checklist-for-lima-to-machu-picchu/)

They get you **to Cusco**. Machu Picchu still needs timed entry ([tuboleto.cultura.pe](https://tuboleto.cultura.pe) / Ministry of Culture), train (PeruRail / Inca Rail), Consettur bus, and usually a guide. The content sites that rank for “how to do Machu Picchu 2026” then hand the traveler to **Yapa Explorers** for the citadel piece ([Yapa vs other operators](https://www.yapaexplorers.com/travel-guides/yapa-explorers-vs-other-machu-picchu-operators-what-makes-them-different/), [The Only Peru Guide](https://www.theonlyperuguide.com/research/cusco-to-machu-picchu-complete-guide/)).

**Implication for PGT**

- You will not beat Peru Hop at “fun bus + hotel pickup + app.”
- You **can** intercept the traveler who already has a Hop pass and now needs MP / Sacred Valley / Inca Trail done by a licensed agency. That is a landing page + WA script: “Arriving with Peru Hop? Send arrival day + group size.”
- Do not compete on Hidroeléctrica cheap-and-chaotic. Mid-tier licensed packages win on tickets + trains + circuit matching. That is PGT’s job.

## 4. What PGT should copy vs ignore

### Copy this week

1. **Permit / availability as a page, not a FAQ afterthought.** Alpaca’s availability URL is a conversion asset. PGT’s Inca Trail FAQ currently says “message us with your target month.” Fine as the close — not as the only content.
2. **Named logistics on trek pages:** circuit number, train class, porter kg, camping vs hotel, “passport name must match permit.” Alpaca does this; SAS does this; Yapa’s reviews praise it.
3. **Office + RUC + license IDs above the fold** on Inca Trail and Machu Picchu hubs — not only in the footer.
4. **Human names on WhatsApp.** SAS puts Noe/David. PGT reviews already name Ubaldina, Ebert, Will, Christian. Put first names on the WA greeting.
5. **Compare 3 products, not 70.** Classic 4D vs Short 2D vs Salkantay 5D. You already have an internal ficha (`pgt/04-producto/fichas/comparativa-inca-trail.md`). Put a public version on `next.`.
6. **Review links that match the listing.** PGT widget: 1,209 TA + 311 Google (synced 2 Sep 2026 from TrustIndex on www). Use those numbers or refresh them — do not round up to “7K+ happy travelers” without a definition.

### Ignore (expensive or brand-damaging)

1. **Cart checkout as the north star.** Drupal staging already made this mistake (Add to cart, no WA). `next.` is correctly WA-first. Keep it.
2. **“#1 on TripAdvisor.”** Alpaca’s own listing rank contradicts the homepage. If PGT does it, a journalist or a competitor screenshot will hurt more than the CTR gain.
3. **Fake scarcity.** Countdown clocks, “2 seats left” without a live allotment, fake permit calendars. Licensed operators get destroyed on TA for this.
4. **Matching Alpaca’s review volume.** That is a 7-year ops + ask-every-guest machine. PGT’s move is **ask every closer this season**, not buy reviews.
5. **Thin blog mass.** EN already has ~450 posts. GSC shows they rank for `costa verde`, `does it snow in peru`, `huaraz` — not `inca trail 4 days`. Stop adding trivia. Fix 8 commercial URLs.
6. **Peru Hop clone / app / hop-on pass.** Wrong product.
7. **Race-to-bottom day-tour pricing on GYG.** Cheap MP day tours are where ticket fraud lives. PGT’s legal page already argues you pay IGV. Price like a formal company.
8. **Waiting for Ricardo to “approve prices.”** He does not gate prices. Use the OTAS sheet / current catalog `priceFrom`. If a number is wrong, hide it (`Request a quote`) rather than freeze the page.

## 5. WhatsApp / lead hacks that fit a licensed operator

No dark patterns. Meta will throttle the number if you spam. Tour-operator playbooks that are actually usable:

- [Scalepact — WA for tour operators](https://scalepact.co/blog/whatsapp-for-tour-operators)
- [CampaignHQ — enquiry to itinerary](https://blog.campaignhq.co/whatsapp-for-tour-operators/)

`next.` already prefills tour + price note (`src/lib/conversion.ts`). That is ahead of WP. The leak is **after the click**.

### Do this (legal, on-brand)

1. **WhatsApp Business profile complete:** legal name, RUC in description, Av. El Sol address, hours, catalog of 8 hero tours, green tick if not already. Separate business number — not a salesperson’s personal phone.
2. **Greeting + 4 buttons, not a novel.**  
   EN example: “Thanks for writing Peru Grand Travel (Cusco, RUC 20603059302). To quote accurately we need: (1) dates (2) how many adults/children (3) Inca Trail / train / full Peru (4) hotel level. A coordinator replies in business hours; after hours we queue you.”
3. **Quick replies** for: Inca Trail permits, Short vs Classic, Salkantay vs Inca Trail, what’s included, payment methods, cancellation.
4. **Away message with a real SLA.** Site currently says “24h WhatsApp support” / “24/7.” If nights are a phone on a desk, say “we reply from 08:00 PET; urgent field support for guests already in Peru.” Overclaiming 24/7 is a TA 1-star waiting to happen.
5. **Lead fields inside the first human reply:** dates, pax, nationality, passport names (Inca Trail), arriving Lima or Cusco, Hop pass yes/no. Template, not interrogation.
6. **Follow-up that adds value, not fake urgency.** Day 1: itinerary PDF. Day 3: “permit window for your month” or circuit explainer. Day 7: “still holding? tell us if dates moved.” Opt-out line. Stay inside Meta’s 24h session; use approved templates outside it.
7. **Click-to-WhatsApp ads (Lizet).** Send to a WA greeting, not a 4,000-word blog. Markets: EN (US is growing per MINCETUR S1 2026) and PT (PGT’s digital strength even if Brazil arrivals dipped).
8. **Post-trip review ask, 24h after return.** One link to TA, one to Google. Named guide. No discount-for-stars (against TA policy and ugly).
9. **Labels in WA Business:** `IT-permit` / `train-MP` / `custom-10d` / `OTA-stealback` / `PT` / `ES` / `EN`. Ricardo does not need to price-gate; sales still needs routing.

### Do not do this

- Scraped contact lists, broadcast “promo Inca Trail.”
- Bots that pretend to be Ubaldina.
- “Only 2 permits left” unless ops is looking at the live Ministry system.
- Asking for passport scans in the first message before the traveler asked for a quote.
- Dual-number chaos (US + PE) without saying who answers which.
